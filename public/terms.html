Terms

A practical color reference, not a professional certification.

ColorFitAI provides informational styling suggestions based on photo pixels and user-confirmed samples. Results are affected by lighting, white balance, filters, makeup, hair dye, screen calibration, and sample placement.

No identity or health inference

The service is not designed to determine ethnicity, race, identity, age, health, attractiveness, or any protected or sensitive trait. Do not use results for employment, insurance, medical, legal, or other high-impact decisions.

Purchasing decisions

Color names and seasonal categories are convenient organizing tools, not objective rules. Always compare the actual item in suitable light and review merchant return policies. We do not guarantee that every recommended color will match every product, dye lot, display, or personal preference.

Paid downloads

Paid packs are browser-generated planning files unlocked by a product-scoped activation code. The download is a worksheet and shopping reference; it is not a human styling certification, body assessment, retail guarantee, or replacement for trying real items in suitable light.

Support and refunds

Contact support with your PayPal receipt ID if activation or download access fails. Refund eligibility remains subject to the checkout provider, applicable law, and the support record for the request.
