Sampling tip

Choose a mid-tone cheek area, natural hair near the crown, and the colored iris. Avoid glare, deep shadow, white of the eye, dyed streaks, and background.

Accuracy boundary

A visual shopping aid, not a verdict about you.

ColorFitAI does not infer ethnicity, identity, health, age, or attractiveness. Camera processing and lighting can shift every sample. Treat the result as a shortlist and compare fabric near your face in daylight before buying.
